A business leader committed to creating lasting value in Nepal's evolving economy.

Suman Sharma is an active investor and multi-venture founder operating at the intersection of hospitality, fashion, and lifestyle in Nepal. With a strong academic foundation in both IT and business, he brings a uniquely analytical approach to entrepreneurship.

As founder of Bamboo Garden Restaurant & Bar, he has built one of the region's recognised dining destinations. His fashion venture Vastra captures the evolving appetite of Nepal's urban youth. As a Goldstar franchise investor, he backs one of Nepal's most trusted footwear brands.

Suman's portfolio reflects a clear thesis: invest in brands that anchor themselves in daily life — food, clothing, and footwear — categories with recurring demand and strong regional tailwinds.
